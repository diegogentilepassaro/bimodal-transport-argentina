THE POPULATION BLOCK IS NOT A ROBUSTNESS RESULT
==========================================================================
  C2 and C3 bin on log_pop_1960, which carries the
  locality-universe measurement error documented in
  diagnostic_pop1960_universe.R AND is the initial level inside
  the outcome chg_log_pop_91_60. Binning on a mismeasured
  variable misassigns districts to bins, and that error is shared
  with the outcome, so those two cells cannot be read as
  robustness. C1 bins on baseline MA and does not have the
  problem. The population block is here because the coauthors
  will ask for it, flagged rather than omitted.
==========================================================================

READING
==========================================================================
  WHAT MOVES. Compare C0 with C1-C3 within an outcome; the sample
  is identical by construction and asserted so, so any movement is
  functional form and nothing else.
  INSTRUMENT STRENGTH IS NOT WHAT CHANGES. Four dummies could in
  principle absorb more of the instruments' variation than one
  slope, which is the mechanism PR #163's ladder measured on
  baseline MA, but here it does not bite: the IV-Both robust F
  spans 12.59 to 17.29 across all 24 cells and it RISES about as
  often as it falls. So the coefficient movements below are not a
  weak-instrument artifact.
  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE. Quintiles are one flexible form among
  many. A result that survives them has survived one departure
  from linearity, not every departure; nothing here tests
  linearity against the data.
