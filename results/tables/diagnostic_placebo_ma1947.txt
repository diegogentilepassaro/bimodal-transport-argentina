HOW TO READ PART 1 (fixed before the numbers were seen):
  clean with ma47_ctrl, dirty with ma60_ctrl -> the post-outcome
    story holds: it is the 1960 POPULATION content of the control
    that revives the rejection, not the presence of a baseline-MA
    level. full47 is then defensible on principle.
  clean with BOTH -> stronger: no baseline-MA level revives the
    rejection, so dropping it is not what produces the null.
  dirty with BOTH -> the post-outcome framing is WRONG. full47's
    null then comes from dropping a control that carries
    legitimate pre-outcome variation, and the honest headline
    placebo is pop47 (keep the log-pop-1960 fix, keep the MA
    baseline, report p = 0.085 as a marginal rejection).
  NOTE on corr(pop47, pop60) above: the closer to 1, the less
  post-outcome content the 1960 control had to begin with, which
  weakens the principled argument whatever the p-values do.
