  READ THIS AS: a decadal growth rate and an urban share of that
  size are not credible for Argentina in 1960. Both are what you
  get when a locality-list denominator meets a full-population
  numerator. The source has no dispersed-rural rows to include,
  so this is a property of the data, not a coding error.
