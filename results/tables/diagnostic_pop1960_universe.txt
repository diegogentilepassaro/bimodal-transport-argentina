  MISALIGNMENT, an alternative explanation for these rows: the
  treatment is the 1960-86 market-access change, so a 1970 or 1980
  baseline is ALREADY PARTLY TREATED and any first-decade response
  is differenced away. An aligned treatment does not exist -- there
  is no 1970 rail vintage and hence no chg_logMA_86_70. So a
  coefficient that falls on the shorter window is consistent with
  BOTH 'the 1960-91 estimate was inflated by the universe mismatch'
  AND 'the response happened mostly in the first decade'. This
  diagnostic does not separate them.
  Caveat throughout: the IV-H first-stage F values above are weak,
  so that column is the least informative of the four.
